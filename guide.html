MenaME Leave Autopilot

Files your leave requests for you.

Reads your punches, finds the missing leaves, and fills them in MenaME. Nothing is sent until you click.

You need

  • Firefox, and the office network or VPN
  • Your MenaME login and the attendance device login
  • Your ID number
Step 1

Install

  1. Drag the .xpi file onto Firefox.
  2. Click Add.
  3. Pin the icon (puzzle-piece → gear → Pin to Toolbar).

Step 2 · once

Your details

Icon → Settings. Enter your ID number, device user number and the device login. Save.

Device user number: on the device go to Report → tick your name → Search → More. It's the number after uid=.
